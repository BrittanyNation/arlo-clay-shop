“Tell me the size, tell me the glaze, tell me what shipping adds. Then I can just enjoy choosing.”

— Sample line, written for this concept

Sizes in real words

Every piece lists its inches and what it holds — a 9-inch bowl that fits a big salad says so, instead of leaving buyers to guess from a photo.

Honest handmade notes

Small glaze variation is stated as a feature of hand-thrown work, with a clear note on what counts as a flaw and what is covered by returns.

Shipping said early

Flat $8 shipping and the free-over-$75 threshold appear on the shop page, the product page, and the cart — the same number in all three places.

Design notes

One collection, one job

The shop page has a single job — help a visitor compare everyday pieces quickly — so size, glaze, use, and price repeat in the same order on every card.

Glaze as the colour system

Cobalt carries the featured piece and primary actions, peach warms the story moments, and sage and sand stay quiet — badges always pair colour with words, never colour alone.

Built for the next page

The card language flows straight into the product page and the cart, so a buyer never has to relearn names, prices, or shipping rules mid-purchase.
