Plain-word questions

When does my card get charged?

In this sample flow, at the moment the order is placed — and the total on the button is the total charged. A live version would repeat that sentence directly above the button.

What if a piece arrives chipped?

Chips and cracks are covered: a photo and one email starts a replacement or refund, your choice. Glaze variation between handmade pieces is not a flaw and is described on every product page before purchase.

Can I change the address after ordering?

Until the shipping label is created, yes — the order update email carries the link. After that, the sample policy says to contact the studio and it will reroute where the carrier allows it.
